Seedream 5.5 prompt writing tips

  • State what should stay unchanged before describing the edit, then change one thing at a time across rounds.
  • Use Seedream 5.5 for long iterative edits, typography layouts, and RGBA or transparent cutout assets.
  • Ask for photoreal materials, lighting, and texture when you need a photographic result.

Seedream 5.5 prompt FAQ

What makes a good Seedream 5.5 prompt?

A good Seedream 5.5 prompt names the subject, scene, composition, style, constraints, and quality requirements in clear language.

Can I copy these Seedream 5.5 prompts?

Yes. Use them as examples, keep attribution when a source is provided, and adapt the subject, brand, scene, or text to your project.

How should I adapt a prompt for Seedream 5.5?

Keep the core structure, then replace the subject, scene, aspect ratio, style, and text rules with your own requirements.
